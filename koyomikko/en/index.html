For parents

Newer titles come with two modes. In Kids mode there is no failing, so a child can play all the way to the end alone. Normal mode is a little more challenging, so parents and kids can take turns.

We keep ads to a minimum and place them where they do not interrupt play. There are no in-app purchases. What each app handles is described in the privacy policy on its support page.

Each app has a short “For parents” screen that explains where the festival comes from. We hope it gives you something to talk about after playing.

Language: the games are in Japanese. Punimoru is also available in English.

We release one new seasonal festival app each month. For feedback or questions, reach us on X (@K2AppDev) or through each app's support page.
